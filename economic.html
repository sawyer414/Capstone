Employment

THE COLLAPSING JOB MARKET

White-Collar Jobs in the Crosshairs

For decades, automation primarily threatened blue-collar manufacturing jobs. Today, AI is upending white-collar professions — legal research, financial analysis, content creation, customer service, and data entry are all being automated at scale. AI bots can perform the same tasks as humans, but far faster and far cheaper.

Large companies have openly acknowledged the concern: replacing human workers with AI too quickly will spike unemployment, drive up homelessness, and ultimately create a worse economic environment for everyone — including the companies doing the replacing. Yet the layoffs continue.

"Every employee and company alike has to worry — if AI can do your job faster and cheaper, the financial incentive to keep you on is disappearing."

Infrastructure

DATA CENTERS: COSTS WITHOUT BENEFITS

The Promise vs. The Reality

When a massive AI data center opens in a small town, residents might hope for new jobs and economic activity. The reality is almost the opposite.

Data centers consume enormous amounts of electricity and water — driving up utility costs for everyone in the region. They are mostly maintained remotely by IT personnel, bringing few or no local jobs.

They also generate massive amounts of heat pollution, contribute to power grid failures, and can depress land values while simultaneously raising taxes in the surrounding area.

  • Higher electricity bills for residents and local businesses
  • Increased water consumption straining local supply
  • Heat pollution contributing to local warming
  • Strain on power grids, leading to outages
  • Land value changes with no job creation to compensate
  • Increased taxes with no corresponding community benefit
  • Almost no local employment created
Market Concentration

THE OPENAI PROBLEM

A Near-Monopoly on Intelligence

OpenAI commands over 40% of all new AI chats, and has publicly discussed acquiring competing data centers. This level of market concentration is dangerous — it limits competition, suppresses innovation, and gives a single private company extraordinary leverage over how the world accesses AI.

Supply Chain

CRITICAL RESOURCE SHORTAGES

Gold & Silicon
AI hardware demands are depleting non-renewable materials, driving up the cost of phones, computers, medical equipment, and military technology.
Clean Water
Data centers use enormous volumes of water for server cooling. In areas with high data center density, local clean water supplies are being strained.
Electricity
AI's power demands are destabilizing power grids in communities near data centers, causing outages and price spikes for ordinary households.
Jobs
As AI replaces workers, a shortage of available employment is emerging — concentrated especially in white-collar sectors once considered safe from automation.
